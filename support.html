Merestone - Support

Merestone shows the environmental designations of Ireland, the United Kingdom, Belgium, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain on a fast native map for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, streamed live from official open data. The statutory core of every country - Natura 2000 and the designations beside it - is free forever; each country's full set is a one-off unlock, or a subscription covers them all.

Need help or found a bug? Email leonfigueira@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings, which fills in your app version for you.

Getting started

  • Tap the Layers button and switch on what you care about - or use a preset.
  • Tap any shape on the map for its official record; star it to save it to your favourites.
  • Search finds sites by name, places, and the grid reference of wherever you are - OS in Britain, Irish Transverse Mercator, Lambert 93, Rijksdriehoek, Lambert 2008, ETRS89 UTM and PT-TM06. Search around here lists everything within a radius of any point - an instant desk study.
  • Settings → Offline maps downloads layers for use with no signal.

Common questions

A layer says it's waiting for zoom. Dense national layers hold back until you zoom in, so the map stays fast. The pill at the bottom tells you how many are waiting - tap Show anyway to force them at any zoom.

How big is "Download everything"? The Offline maps screen quotes the figure for whatever you are about to download, per layer and per country, before it starts - that is the number to trust, because it moves as layers are added. Everything in every country is far more than anyone wants; one country is a small fraction of it. Use Wi-Fi, expect a few sittings, and download just the layers you need if you would rather. Nothing already downloaded is ever lost.

Which countries are covered? Ireland, the United Kingdom - England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, each from its own publisher - Belgium in all three regions, Germany, France including Corsica, the Netherlands, Portugal with Madeira and the Azores, and Spain including the Canaries and the Balearics. Some countries also carry regional depth: Germany in eight Länder, Spain in four comunidades - Castilla y León, the Comunitat Valenciana, Catalunya and Andalucía - and the Netherlands through the waterschappen. The Layers panel names the publisher behind every layer, and the app says where a country's coverage stops rather than leaving you to find out.

How do I get the Watch app? Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and install Merestone. On your wrist it shows the habitat you're standing in, or the nearest designations with distances and grid references.

My location isn't showing on the Mac. Allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, then click the location button again.

Is this the same data as the government sites? Yes - Merestone streams from the same official services, live. It's an independent app, not affiliated with Defra, Natural England or the MAGIC service, and boundaries should always be verified against official sources before professional reliance.
