Merestone - Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026, updated 13 September 2026 for Merestone 2.3

Merestone needs no account and collects no personal data unless you choose to sign in. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The optional Merestone account, and exactly what it stores, is described below.

Your location

If you allow location access, your position is used on your device to centre the map, show grid references, and (on Apple Watch) find the designations around you. It is never stored by Merestone or sent to me.

Map data requests

Merestone streams its layers directly from the official public services that publish them, and uses Apple Maps as the base map. Those services are, by country:

  • United Kingdom - Natural England, Historic England, the Environment Agency, the Forestry Commission, NatureScot, SEPA, Natural Resources Wales, Cadw, DAERA and planning.data.gov.uk.
  • Ireland - the NPWS, the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage, Geological Survey Ireland, the EPA, the National Monuments Service, the NIAH, Tailte Éireann, Teagasc and Sport Ireland.
  • France - the Muséum national d'Histoire naturelle (INPN), IGN Géoplateforme, the Géoportail de l'urbanisme, Géorisques (BRGM), Sandre/OFB, the ONF and INAO.
  • Spain - the Generalitat Valenciana (ICV), the Generalitat de Catalunya, the Junta de Andalucía (REDIAM) and the European Environment Agency.
  • Portugal - the ICNF, the APA, the Direção-Geral do Território, the Açores regional government and the European Environment Agency.
  • Netherlands - PDOK, the Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, the Rijksdienst voor het Cultureel Erfgoed, the Kadaster, Rijkswaterstaat and the waterschappen via Het Waterschapshuis.
  • Belgium - the Service public de Wallonie (WalOnMap), Bruxelles Environnement, urban.brussels, Digitaal Vlaanderen (Geopunt), the Agentschap Natuur en Bos, the Vlaamse Milieumaatschappij and the agentschap Onroerend Erfgoed.
  • Germany - the European Environment Agency, LANUK Nordrhein-Westfalen, the Bayerisches Landesamt für Umwelt, the LUBW Baden-Württemberg, the Sächsisches Landesamt für Umwelt, the NLWKN Niedersachsen, the HLNUG Hessen, Geoportal Berlin and the BUKEA Hamburg.
  • Switzerland - the federal geoportal geo.admin.ch (for the Federal Office for the Environment, the Federal Office of Culture, the Federal Office for Civil Protection, the Federal Roads Office, the Federal Office for Spatial Development and the other federal offices it publishes for), geodienste.ch for the cantons' data, and the European Environment Agency.

When the app requests data, the service being asked receives the same standard information any map app sends: your IP address and the area of the map being loaded. It only ever hears from you for layers you have switched on. Merestone adds nothing to identify you, and none of those requests pass through any server of mine. The app also downloads two small public files from this site (the list of layer updates and service notices), which carry nothing about you.

What stays on your device

Notes, folders, saved presets, settings and offline downloads are stored on your device. Favourites are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, copied between your own devices through your iCloud account; I cannot see them. CSV exports go wherever you choose to share them. Shared links contain only the map coordinates and layer of the feature being shared.

Purchases

Purchases - a one-off country unlock, or the subscription that covers every country - are handled entirely by Apple. I receive no personal information about you from them.

The optional Merestone account (version 2.3 and later)

You never need an account to use or buy Merestone. If you choose to sign in (Settings, Merestone account), the app sends your email address to merestonemaps.com, which emails you a six-digit code. The app keeps the resulting sign-in token in your device's keychain. merestonemaps.com stores your email address, the date you signed in, your language and a record of each sign-in session. What an account holds on merestonemaps.com (a purchase made on the website) then also unlocks in the app.

If you choose to link your App Store purchases, the app sends Apple's signed record of each one to merestonemaps.com: the transaction number, the product, the purchase date, a subscription's end date, and whether Apple has refunded or revoked it. Apple then tells merestonemaps.com about refunds and renewals of those purchases, and they are checked with Apple each night. This is never your Apple Account, your name or your payment details, and it is used only so the same purchases work on merestonemaps.com.

You can sign out at any time, and delete the account and everything it holds from Settings in the app or from your account page on merestonemaps.com. The full details of what the website stores are in the merestonemaps.com privacy policy.
